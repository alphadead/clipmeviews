Process

How One Video Becomes a Thousand Posts

No magic. Just a system that compounds while you sleep.

01

You send. We sort.

Drop your long-form content into our pipeline. Podcasts, interviews, talks, livestreams — anything you've already made. No extra filming, no extra prep. You keep creating exactly how you do now.

02

We find the moments worth multiplying.

Our team watches every second and pulls the clips that actually deserve to travel. Not "fine" content. Stop-the-scroll content. The lines someone would quote to a friend. If a moment won't earn a share, it doesn't make the cut.

03

One clip becomes ten.

Each moment gets recut, recaptioned, and rebuilt for the platform it's going to. What works on Reels dies on X. What lands on TikTok flops on Shorts. We make native versions for each feed — not lazy reposts with the wrong aspect ratio.

04

Then we post. A thousand times.

Your clips drop across our network of fan and topic accounts on every major platform. Same content, different angles, parallel reach. Your audience stops finding you and starts seeing you everywhere they scroll.

The whole system runs in the background. You film once. We make it impossible to miss.

FAQs

  • 100% real, human-run accounts with genuine audiences. No bots, no fake engagement. Every view comes from a real person on a real account within our distribution network.
